# Collaboration expectations

Treat contributors and maintainers with respect. Discuss concrete behavior and
evidence. Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats and publishing private data
are unacceptable. Keep public examples synthetic and respect disclosure boundaries.

Repository maintainers may edit/remove harmful content or restrict participation.
Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through an established
channel; do not publish personal information in an issue. These expectations also
apply to content produced by AI agents acting for contributors.
